09 — PURE FABRIC MACRO / T-SHIRT OR POLO T-SHIRT

Generate exactly one ultra-photorealistic fabric macro photograph of the actual garment material.

OUTPUT

• exactly one image
• square format
• exactly 1254 × 1254 px
• pure fabric study
• no collage, caption, border or watermark
• no added text
• no model
• no logo unless a small genuine trim edge is intentionally included and exactly supported

CATEGORY AUTO-DETECTION

Inspect every supplied actual-product photograph before generation.

Classify the garment as:

• T-SHIRT — crew neck, round neck, V-neck or equivalent neckline without a polo collar and buttoned placket.
• POLO T-SHIRT — structured or knitted polo collar with a placket and one or more buttons.

Preserve the detected category exactly.

Never convert:
• a T-shirt into a polo
• a polo into a T-shirt
• either garment into a Henley, woven shirt, sweatshirt or another category
• the product into a hybrid garment

SOURCE OF TRUTH

Use every supplied photograph of the current product as the sole source of truth for:

• garment category
• colour and undertone
• silhouette and fit
• proportions
• neckline or collar
• collar tipping or colour blocking
• placket construction
• exact visible button count
• sleeve shape and cuff construction
• fabric texture, knit structure, drape and sheen
• stitching, seams and reinforcement
• chest branding, labels, artwork and all genuine trims

ARTICLE IMMUTABILITY — ABSOLUTE

The article is final.

Never redesign, modernise, simplify, recolour, resize, reposition, replace, add or remove any permanent garment element.

Preserve exactly:

• logo characters and spelling
• typography and letter spacing
• logo proportions, orientation, colour, placement and scale
• embroidery or print texture
• thread direction and edge character
• permanent neck labels
• collar, neckline, placket, buttons, cuffs, hems and seams

Never:

• retype or redraw branding
• use a generic replacement font
• mirror or relocate branding
• invent missing characters
• substitute another brand
• add styling details not present in the product photographs
• remove genuine permanent details

If the supplied photographs do not support exact reproduction, reject and regenerate. Never approximate.

FABRIC SOURCE OF TRUTH

Use the supplied close product photographs to determine:

• exact fabric colour and undertone
• fibre appearance
• knit or weave structure
• yarn thickness
• stitch density
• surface softness
• drape
• sheen level
• natural irregularity

Never invent a generic pique, jersey or interlock texture.

MACRO COMPOSITION

• fabric arranged in natural soft waves or folds
• one foreground ridge in crisp focus
• surrounding folds retain enough detail to identify structure
• close enough to show yarn and knit behaviour
• not so close that the textile becomes abstract or biologically textured
• no repeated cloned pattern
• no impossible geometric symmetry

OPTIONAL TRIM

A small portion of genuine collar, cuff, neckband, tipping or hem may enter one edge of the frame only when it helps identify the product.

Any visible trim must be exact.

LIGHTING

• soft raking light revealing surface relief
• accurate colour
• realistic highlight roll-off
• no glitter
• no synthetic shine
• no velvet-like appearance unless the actual fabric is velvet
• no exaggerated fibre fuzz

BACKGROUND

The entire frame should be dominated by the actual fabric. No tabletop, props or unrelated background should be prominent.

FINAL REJECTION CHECK

Reject and regenerate unless:

• knit or weave structure matches the actual product
• colour and undertone are accurate
• drape is physically plausible
• texture is non-repeating
• no foreign textile structure is introduced
• output is exactly 1254 × 1254 px
