04 — CINEMATIC CONSTRUCTION DETAIL MACRO / T-SHIRT OR POLO T-SHIRT

Generate exactly one cinematic construction-detail macro photograph of the actual garment.

OUTPUT

• exactly one image
• square format
• exactly 1254 × 1254 px
• ultra-photorealistic
• one continuous composition
• no collage, inset, caption, border or watermark
• no added text outside genuine garment branding

CATEGORY AUTO-DETECTION

Inspect every supplied actual-product photograph before generation.

Classify the garment as:

• T-SHIRT — crew neck, round neck, V-neck or equivalent neckline without a polo collar and buttoned placket.
• POLO T-SHIRT — structured or knitted polo collar with a placket and one or more buttons.

Preserve the detected category exactly.

Never convert:
• a T-shirt into a polo
• a polo into a T-shirt
• either garment into a Henley, woven shirt, sweatshirt or another category
• the product into a hybrid garment

SOURCE OF TRUTH

Use every supplied photograph of the current product as the sole source of truth for:

• garment category
• colour and undertone
• silhouette and fit
• proportions
• neckline or collar
• collar tipping or colour blocking
• placket construction
• exact visible button count
• sleeve shape and cuff construction
• fabric texture, knit structure, drape and sheen
• stitching, seams and reinforcement
• chest branding, labels, artwork and all genuine trims

ARTICLE IMMUTABILITY — ABSOLUTE

The article is final.

Never redesign, modernise, simplify, recolour, resize, reposition, replace, add or remove any permanent garment element.

Preserve exactly:

• logo characters and spelling
• typography and letter spacing
• logo proportions, orientation, colour, placement and scale
• embroidery or print texture
• thread direction and edge character
• permanent neck labels
• collar, neckline, placket, buttons, cuffs, hems and seams

Never:

• retype or redraw branding
• use a generic replacement font
• mirror or relocate branding
• invent missing characters
• substitute another brand
• add styling details not present in the product photographs
• remove genuine permanent details

If the supplied photographs do not support exact reproduction, reject and regenerate. Never approximate.

MACRO SUBJECT

Select one compact area containing the highest-value construction details supported by the product photographs.

Suitable details include:

For a T-shirt:
• neckband and neckline seam
• shoulder reinforcement
• sleeve hem
• bottom hem
• embroidery or print edge
• seam intersection

For a polo T-shirt:
• collar edge and tipping
• collar-to-placket junction
• placket stitching
• button and buttonhole construction
• sleeve cuff and tipping
• embroidered chest branding
• permanent neck label only when fully supported

Show only details genuinely present on the product.

COMPOSITION

• cinematic oblique macro angle
• controlled shallow depth of field
• one main detail tack-sharp
• surrounding garment softly recedes
• no extreme blur that hides construction
• no duplicated textile surfaces
• no impossible fabric folds

SURFACE AND LIGHTING

• neutral premium studio surface
• directional soft light revealing stitch depth, rib structure and embroidery
• realistic shadows
• accurate colour and undertone
• no exaggerated gloss
• no metallic-looking cotton
• no artificial embossed texture

BRANDING SAFETY

If branding appears, it must remain exact and fully legible.

Do not include partially invented label text or distorted logo fragments. When exact reproduction is uncertain, frame the macro around construction without exposing unsupported text.

FINAL REJECTION CHECK

Reject and regenerate unless:

• the selected construction area exists in the source product
• all seams, stitches, buttons, trims and tipping are structurally correct
• fabric texture is plausible and non-repeating
• branding is exact when visible
• category is preserved
• output is exactly 1254 × 1254 px
