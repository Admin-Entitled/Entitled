SLEEVE PRESENTATION — ABSOLUTE / MATCH PROMPT 01
====================================================================================================

The back-view sleeves must use the SAME presentation philosophy as Prompt 01 front.

THE TWO HIGHEST-PRIORITY SLEEVE REQUIREMENTS ARE:

1. PRESERVE THE ACTUAL SOURCE-DERIVED SHORT SLEEVE LENGTH.
2. CREATE ONE CLEAR, RULER-STRAIGHT, DELIBERATELY PRESSED PRESENTATION FOLD ON EACH SLEEVE.

Both conditions must be satisfied simultaneously.

A result satisfying only one condition is incorrect.

The sleeves MAY open outward or diagonally as much as reasonably necessary to maintain the straight fold.

Do NOT force the sleeves close to the torso.

Do NOT force the sleeves vertically downward.

Sleeve angle is flexible.

Sleeve length and dominant-fold straightness are not flexible.

PRIMARY SLEEVE FOLD — STRAIGHT PRESSED EDGE / ABSOLUTE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each sleeve must contain ONE dominant, deliberate, pressed flat-lay presentation fold.

This required fold is NOT:

• the natural armhole seam
• the sleeve attachment seam
• the curved underarm transition
• a wrinkle
• a ripple
• a soft drape line
• an approximate crease

The natural armhole seam may remain curved where required by real garment construction.

THE REQUIRED PRESENTATION FOLD IS A SEPARATE STRAIGHT FOLD ACROSS THE SLEEVE FABRIC.

The dominant fold on each sleeve must:

• form one continuous ruler-straight line
• remain straight from upper endpoint to lower endpoint
• remain visually clear
• remain smooth and deliberately pressed
• contain no convex bow
• contain no concave bow
• contain no arc
• contain no S-curve
• contain no waviness
• contain no rounded middle section
• contain no competing secondary fold
• mirror the opposite sleeve closely

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULER TEST — ABSOLUTE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Imagine placing one rigid straight ruler over the complete dominant fold.

The entire fold must align with that ruler.

If any part of the fold:

• bows inward
• bows outward
• curves
• arcs
• bends
• wanders away from the straight path

the image fails.

Even a slight visible curve is unacceptable.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOLD CONSTRUCTION — PHYSICALLY REALISTIC
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The straight fold must result from believable folded fabric geometry, not a digitally drawn line.

The sleeve fabric should change plane cleanly along the fold and create:

• subtle physical depth
• realistic fabric thickness
• restrained highlight/shadow separation
• a crisp structural edge
• a professionally prepared ecommerce flat-lay appearance

Do not invent piping, seams, printed lines or artificial edges.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLEEVE ORIENTATION — FLEXIBLE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The sleeves may:

• open outward
• sit diagonally outward
• spread farther from the torso
• use a moderate downward angle

Choose whichever position best preserves:

1. ruler-straight dominant fold
2. actual source-derived short sleeve length
3. left/right mirror symmetry
4. correct cuff / sleeve-opening construction

A more open sleeve is preferable to a curved fold.

Never compromise fold straightness merely to keep sleeves close to the body.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEFT / RIGHT MIRROR LOCK
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Both sleeves must closely mirror each other in:

• sleeve length
• sleeve width
• sleeve-opening width
• spread angle
• dominant-fold angle
• dominant-fold straightness
• upper fold endpoint
• lower fold endpoint
• fold depth
• visual tension
• cuff presentation

One sleeve must not appear straighter, longer, wider or more open than the other.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLEEVE PRIORITY ORDER — ABSOLUTE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When sleeve requirements compete, follow this hierarchy:

1. RULER-STRAIGHT DOMINANT PRESENTATION FOLD
2. ORIGINAL SOURCE-DERIVED SHORT SLEEVE LENGTH
3. LEFT/RIGHT MIRROR SYMMETRY
4. CORRECT SLEEVE WIDTH AND SHAPE
5. CORRECT CUFF / SLEEVE-OPENING CONSTRUCTION
6. CORRECT TIPPING OR TRIMS
7. CLEAN PRESSED PRESENTATION
8. SLEEVE ANGLE
9. PROXIMITY TO THE TORSO

If opening the sleeves wider creates a straighter fold:

OPEN THEM WIDER.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ABSOLUTE SLEEVE REJECTION CONDITIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Reject and regenerate if:

• either dominant fold curves at any point
• either dominant fold bows inward or outward
• either dominant fold forms an arc or S-shape
• either dominant fold follows the natural curved armhole seam
• either dominant fold appears as a soft wrinkle
• left and right fold geometry differs materially
• either sleeve length differs from the source
• sleeve length changes because of orientation
• one sleeve is visibly longer, wider or more open than the other
• cuff or sleeve-opening construction is distorted
• sleeves are forced close to the torso at the expense of fold straightness
