SLEEVE PRESENTATION — ABSOLUTE / NON-NEGOTIABLE
===============================================

The sleeve presentation is a defining requirement of this image.

THE TWO HIGHEST-PRIORITY SLEEVE REQUIREMENTS ARE:

1. PRESERVE THE ACTUAL SHORT SLEEVE LENGTH OF THE SUPPLIED PRODUCT.
2. CREATE ONE CLEAR, RULER-STRAIGHT, DELIBERATELY PRESSED FOLD ON EACH SLEEVE.

BOTH conditions must be satisfied simultaneously.

A result satisfying only one condition is incorrect.

The sleeves MAY open outward or diagonally as much as reasonably necessary to achieve the required straight-fold presentation.

Do NOT force the sleeves close to the torso.

Do NOT force the sleeves vertically downward.

Sleeve angle is flexible.

Sleeve length and fold straightness are not flexible.

====================================================================================================

=====================================

Never use:

• sleeve-to-chest-width proportion

Never:

• extend the sleeve toward the elbow
• increase sleeve length to create a cleaner straight line
• increase sleeve length because the sleeve is angled downward

SLEEVE POSITION and SLEEVE LENGTH are independent variables.

Changing sleeve position must NEVER change sleeve length.

====================================================================================================
PRIMARY SLEEVE FOLD — STRAIGHT PRESSED EDGE / ABSOLUTE
======================================================

The required visible fold on each sleeve is a DELIBERATE PRESSED FLAT-LAY FOLD EDGE.

THIS DISTINCTION IS CRITICAL.

The required fold is NOT:

• the armhole seam
• the sleeve attachment seam
• the natural underarm curve
• a natural drape line
• a wrinkle
• a ripple
• a soft crease
• a curved transition between body and sleeve

The natural armhole seam may remain curved because that curvature belongs to the actual garment construction.

THE REQUIRED STRAIGHT FOLD IS A SEPARATE PRESENTATION FOLD CREATED ACROSS THE SLEEVE FABRIC.

====================================================================================================
FOLD GEOMETRY — NON-NEGOTIABLE
==============================

Each sleeve must contain exactly ONE dominant presentation fold.

That dominant fold must form ONE SINGLE STRAIGHT LINE SEGMENT.

It must be:

• ruler-straight
• visually clear
• continuous
• deliberate
• crisp
• clean
• professionally pressed
• symmetrical with the opposite sleeve

The fold must have:

• NO convex curve
• NO concave curve
• NO bow
• NO arc
• NO S-shape
• NO rounded middle
• NO waviness
• NO lateral wandering
• NO multiple competing crease lines

A gentle curve is NOT acceptable.

A natural-looking curve is NOT acceptable.

A visually pleasing curve is NOT acceptable.

The dominant presentation fold itself must be geometrically straight.

====================================================================================================
STRAIGHT-LINE ENDPOINT LOCK
===========================

Treat each required sleeve fold as a line defined by exactly TWO endpoints.

POINT A — UPPER FOLD ENDPOINT

Located on the visible sleeve panel, separate from the natural curved armhole seam.

POINT B — LOWER FOLD ENDPOINT

Located at or immediately adjacent to the sleeve opening / cuff region.

The visible pressed fold must connect POINT A directly to POINT B.

A SINGLE STRAIGHT VECTOR must connect these two points.

The physical fabric fold must visually follow this straight vector.

Nothing between POINT A and POINT B may deviate from that straight path.

====================================================================================================
RULER TEST — ABSOLUTE
=====================

Imagine physically placing a rigid straight ruler directly over the dominant sleeve fold.

The ENTIRE fold from POINT A to POINT B must align with the ruler.

No section may:

• move away from the ruler
• curve toward the torso
• curve away from the torso
• bow inward
• bow outward
• round off
• arc
• bend

If the entire fold cannot be covered by one straight ruler:

THE IMAGE FAILS.

Even a small visible bow is a failure.

• a digitally drawn line
• artificial dark piping

The fold should create:

• subtle physical depth
• realistic fabric thickness
• restrained highlight/shadow separation
• a crisp structural edge
• a professional flat-lay presentation

Imagine that a professional ecommerce stylist physically prepared the garment as follows:

1. place garment flat
2. preserve the genuine short sleeve dimensions
3. open each sleeve outward sufficiently
4. align the sleeve fabric manually
5. use a straight guide to establish the fold
6. press the sleeve along that straight guide
7. photograph the garment without disturbing the fold

The generated result must visually reproduce that preparation.

====================================================================================================
ARMHOLE CURVE VS PRESENTATION FOLD — CRITICAL DISTINCTION
=========================================================

ARMHOLE CURVE = ALLOWED

DOMINANT PRESENTATION FOLD CURVE = FORBIDDEN

Do NOT attempt to convert the natural armhole seam into the required fold.

Do NOT follow the armhole curvature when creating the presentation fold.

The straight fold must appear as a separate visible pressed edge across the sleeve panel.

================================
