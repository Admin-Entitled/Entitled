02 — PREMIUM FOLDED PRODUCT HERO / T-SHIRT OR POLO T-SHIRT

Generate exactly one premium folded-product ecommerce hero photograph.

OUTPUT

• exactly one image
• square format
• exactly 1254 × 1254 px
• ultra-photorealistic
• no collage, caption, border or watermark
• no added text outside genuine garment branding

CATEGORY AUTO-DETECTION

Inspect every supplied actual-product photograph before generation.

Classify the garment as:

• T-SHIRT — crew neck, round neck, V-neck or equivalent neckline without a polo collar and buttoned placket.
• POLO T-SHIRT — structured or knitted polo collar with a placket and one or more buttons.

Preserve the detected category exactly.

Never convert:
• a T-shirt into a polo
• a polo into a T-shirt
• either garment into a Henley, woven shirt, sweatshirt or another category
• the product into a hybrid garment

SOURCE OF TRUTH

Use every supplied photograph of the current product as the sole source of truth for:

• garment category
• colour and undertone
• silhouette and fit
• proportions
• neckline or collar
• collar tipping or colour blocking
• placket construction
• exact visible button count
• sleeve shape and cuff construction
• fabric texture, knit structure, drape and sheen
• stitching, seams and reinforcement
• chest branding, labels, artwork and all genuine trims

ARTICLE IMMUTABILITY — ABSOLUTE

The article is final.

Never redesign, modernise, simplify, recolour, resize, reposition, replace, add or remove any permanent garment element.

Preserve exactly:

• logo characters and spelling
• typography and letter spacing
• logo proportions, orientation, colour, placement and scale
• embroidery or print texture
• thread direction and edge character
• permanent neck labels
• collar, neckline, placket, buttons, cuffs, hems and seams

Never:

• retype or redraw branding
• use a generic replacement font
• mirror or relocate branding
• invent missing characters
• substitute another brand
• add styling details not present in the product photographs
• remove genuine permanent details

If the supplied photographs do not support exact reproduction, reject and regenerate. Never approximate.

COMPOSITION

Present the exact garment neatly folded on a refined matte surface.

The fold must showcase the product's strongest identifying details:

For a T-shirt:
• neckline or neckband
• upper chest
• logo or artwork when present
• sleeve edge
• fabric texture

For a polo T-shirt:
• complete collar
• exact collar tipping or colour blocking
• placket
• exact buttons
• one sleeve cuff with its genuine tipping
• upper chest logo or branding
• permanent neck label when naturally visible

The garment must remain physically plausible. Do not create impossible folds, duplicated sleeves, broken seams or distorted proportions.

STYLE

• luxury menswear ecommerce
• restrained, minimal and product-first
• premium editorial polish without decorative clutter
• no jewellery, watch, footwear, sunglasses, books, flowers or unrelated props

SURFACE AND BACKGROUND

• matte stone, brushed metal, refined mineral slab or smooth neutral studio platform
• colour must complement the garment without changing its perceived colour
• background remains understated and secondary
• no visible room clutter

LIGHTING

• soft directional studio light
• controlled natural shadow beneath the folded garment
• accurate fabric colour
• visible knit structure and stitching
• no fake shine
• no excessive contrast

FRAMING

• deliberate three-quarter overhead angle
• folded garment occupies approximately 75–85% of the canvas
• collar or neckline and branding remain clearly readable
• no accidental crop of relevant product details

FINAL REJECTION CHECK

Reject and regenerate unless:

• the garment remains the exact supplied product
• fold geometry is realistic
• category is preserved
• branding, collar, cuffs, placket and buttons are exact
• no product detail is invented
• output is exactly 1254 × 1254 px
