# ENTITLED VISUAL SYSTEM

## 1. PURPOSE

This file defines the permanent visual language for all ENTITLED image generation.

Use it as the master art-direction reference for:
- ecommerce product imagery
- studio model imagery
- editorial campaigns
- social media creatives
- website banners
- product-detail imagery
- fabric and construction macros
- lifestyle imagery

This file controls the visual world around the product.

It does NOT override the actual supplied product photographs.
The supplied product photographs always remain the sole source of truth for the garment itself.

---

## 2. BRAND POSITIONING

ENTITLED is a premium, members-oriented menswear brand.

The visual identity must feel:
- exclusive
- controlled
- modern
- understated
- confident
- precise
- premium
- editorial
- masculine
- design-conscious

The brand must never feel:
- mass-market
- loud
- juvenile
- gimmicky
- overly trendy
- discount-led
- cluttered
- synthetic
- generic AI-generated
- generic Shopify/D2C

ENTITLED should feel like a distinct fashion house with its own visual universe.

---

## 3. VISUAL ATTITUDE

The visual tone should be:

Cold confidence with restrained luxury.

Prefer:
- disciplined composition
- strong negative space
- architectural balance
- controlled lighting
- premium material rendering
- subtle tension
- deliberate framing
- minimal visual noise
- refined restraint

Avoid:
- excessive props
- decorative clutter
- loud backgrounds
- oversaturated colors
- aggressive HDR
- artificial glow
- fake luxury tropes
- random fashion accessories
- unnecessary typography
- over-styling

---

## 4. CORE COLOR SYSTEM

Primary palette:

- Obsidian: #0B0B0C
- Graphite: #17181B
- Warm Ivory: #F2ECE2
- Oxblood: #5B0A19
- Muted Gold: #C8B58A

Standard catalogue background:

- #EDEBE8
- RGB 237, 235, 232
- sRGB

When a prompt specifies the catalogue background, use this exact color with no visible tint shift.

Do not introduce competing brand colors unless explicitly requested.

---

## 5. BACKGROUND LANGUAGE

### Ecommerce / Catalogue
Use:
- clean
- flat
- seamless
- distraction-free
- neutral
- premium

Preferred catalogue background:
- exact #EDEBE8 when specified

Do not:
- add gradients unless requested
- add visible texture
- add random studio corners
- add colored cast
- introduce props

### Editorial / Campaign
Backgrounds may be:
- architectural
- monochrome
- dark studio
- concrete
- stone
- brushed metal
- minimal urban
- restrained luxury interiors

The environment should support the garment, never compete with it.

---

## 6. LIGHTING SYSTEM

ENTITLED lighting should feel controlled and expensive.

Preferred:
- soft directional light
- large diffused sources
- clean shadow structure
- realistic material response
- subtle dimensionality
- restrained contrast
- accurate garment color

For darker campaigns:
- cinematic but readable
- deep blacks without crushed fabric detail
- controlled highlights
- no neon contamination

For ecommerce:
- neutral white balance
- even exposure
- no dramatic color cast
- faithful material rendering

Avoid:
- excessive rim light
- fake volumetric haze
- glowing edges
- harsh flash aesthetic unless specifically requested
- extreme bloom
- overexposure
- crushed blacks

---

## 7. COMPOSITION SYSTEM

ENTITLED images should feel intentional.

Prefer:
- one dominant subject
- clear visual hierarchy
- controlled symmetry or deliberate asymmetry
- generous breathing room
- strong alignment
- geometric framing
- editorial cropping
- product-first composition

Avoid:
- busy scenes
- random objects
- multiple competing focal points
- unnecessary text
- visual gimmicks
- awkward cropping
- excessive empty deadspace without purpose

---

## 8. PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE

Product images should feel like luxury ecommerce rather than marketplace photography.

Characteristics:
- clean
- exact
- premium
- sharp
- realistic
- professionally prepared
- highly controlled

Garments should appear:
- properly shaped
- naturally draped
- professionally steam-ironed where appropriate
- structurally accurate
- free from accidental distortion

The image must preserve the authentic material, construction and proportions.

---

## 9. MODEL PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE

Models should feel:
- adult
- premium
- confident
- understated
- contemporary
- natural
- editorial

Preferred expression:
- neutral
- composed
- restrained
- self-assured

Preferred posing:
- natural standing
- controlled movement
- subtle three-quarter turns
- minimal gesturing
- no exaggerated fashion posing

Avoid:
- influencer-style posing
- exaggerated smiles
- loud accessories
- costume-like styling
- unrealistic body proportions
- excessive muscle emphasis
- dramatic runway theatrics unless specifically requested

The garment must remain the hero.

---

## 10. STYLING LANGUAGE

Styling should be restrained.

Preferred:
- tonal trousers
- premium neutral bottoms
- minimal footwear
- simple layering only when requested
- no unnecessary accessories

If the garment is the primary product, never introduce styling that hides:
- logo
- artwork
- collar
- neckline
- placket
- sleeves
- hem
- key construction details

---

## 11. EDITORIAL INFLUENCE

The visual benchmark should draw from principles commonly associated with:
- European luxury fashion editorials
- restrained contemporary menswear
- architectural minimalism
- premium fashion catalogues
- quiet-luxury image making

Do not directly clone any specific brand campaign, photographer, artwork or copyrighted composition.

ENTITLED must remain visually original.

---

## 12. FABRIC AND MATERIAL RENDERING

Fabric must look:
- tactile
- authentic
- detailed
- natural
- physically plausible

Preserve:
- weave
- knit structure
- thickness
- weight
- drape
- sheen
- nap
- texture
- stitching

Do not:
- smooth away texture
- invent fibers
- add fake lint
- exaggerate grain
- change material type
- make cotton look synthetic
- make matte fabric glossy

---

## 13. IMAGE QUALITY

Target:
- ultra-photorealistic
- premium ecommerce/editorial quality
- clean edge definition
- realistic texture
- realistic shadows
- natural perspective
- accurate anatomy when models are used
- accurate product geometry
- no AI artifacts

Avoid:
- warped seams
- melted buttons
- malformed hands
- inconsistent labels
- duplicated details
- fake embroidery
- blurred branding
- distorted typography

---

## 14. TYPOGRAPHY IN GRAPHICS

When text is genuinely required:
- minimal
- precise
- highly legible
- restrained
- premium

Avoid:
- decorative fonts
- oversized marketing slogans
- fake luxury serif overload
- unnecessary captions
- AI-generated replacement logos

Use the supplied official ENTITLED logo asset whenever branding artwork is required.

---

## 15. SOCIAL AND CAMPAIGN VISUALS

For social/editorial content:
- maintain strong product recognition
- preserve ENTITLED restraint
- use bolder art direction without sacrificing product fidelity
- keep the image premium and ownable

A campaign image may be more dramatic than ecommerce imagery, but it must still feel unmistakably ENTITLED.

---

## 16. CONSISTENCY RULE

All ENTITLED images generated across different products should appear to belong to the same brand universe.

Maintain consistency in:
- lighting philosophy
- background philosophy
- visual restraint
- image cleanliness
- framing discipline
- material realism
- model attitude
- premium tone

Do not let each prompt invent an entirely new aesthetic.

---

## 17. SOURCE PRIORITY

When generating an image, follow this hierarchy:

1. Actual supplied product photographs = absolute garment truth
2. ENTITLED_IMAGE_RULES.md = immutable product-fidelity rules
3. This file = visual/art-direction system
4. Individual numbered prompt = specific shot/composition instruction
5. Optional supplied composition reference = layout reference only unless explicitly stated otherwise

If any lower-priority instruction conflicts with a higher-priority source, follow the higher-priority source.

---

## 18. FINAL VISUAL TEST

Before accepting an image, it should pass all five questions:

1. Does this look like ENTITLED rather than generic AI fashion imagery?
2. Is the garment visually faithful to the supplied product?
3. Is the composition disciplined and premium?
4. Is the material believable and accurately rendered?
5. Could this image sit next to other ENTITLED images without looking unrelated?

If any answer is no, the image should be corrected.
