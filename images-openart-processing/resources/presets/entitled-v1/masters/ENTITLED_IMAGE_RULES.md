# ENTITLED IMAGE RULES

## 1. PURPOSE

This file contains the permanent non-negotiable rules for all ENTITLED product image generation.

These rules protect product identity.

They apply to every image type unless a specific instruction explicitly concerns only composition or camera treatment.

The actual supplied product photographs are always the sole visual source of truth for the garment.

---

## 2. PRODUCT IDENTITY LOCK — ABSOLUTE

The product is final.

Never redesign, reinterpret, improve, simplify, modernize or creatively modify the garment.

Preserve exactly:
- garment category
- silhouette
- fit
- proportions
- body length
- width
- shoulder structure
- sleeve length
- sleeve width
- neckline
- collar
- placket
- buttons
- cuffs
- seams
- hem
- stitching
- panels
- ribbing
- trims
- piping
- tipping
- labels
- patches
- embroidery
- prints
- graphics
- logo
- branding placement
- fabric
- texture
- color
- undertone
- finish
- drape

No creative freedom is permitted for garment design.

---

## 3. SOURCE OF TRUTH

Use every supplied photograph of the current product as the sole source of truth for the garment.

Cross-reference all available views before generation.

Do not rely on:
- generic garment knowledge
- previous generations
- another product
- another colorway
- memory of a previous article
- a composition reference
- a model reference
- a size-chart image

Reference images used for composition must not alter the actual product.

---

## 4. CATEGORY LOCK

Detect the garment category from the actual product photographs and preserve it exactly.

Never convert:
- T-shirt into polo
- polo into T-shirt
- shirt into overshirt
- shirt into jacket
- round neck into Henley
- short sleeve into long sleeve
- woven fabric into knit
- knit into woven
- any product into a hybrid category

---

## 5. COLOR LOCK — ABSOLUTE

The garment color and undertone must match the supplied product photographs.

Never:
- recolor
- desaturate
- oversaturate
- warm the garment
- cool the garment
- shift hue
- make sky blue appear grey
- make cream appear white
- make black appear blue
- alter contrast in a way that changes perceived product color

Lighting may shape the product, but must not materially change its perceived real-world color.

---

## 6. PROPORTION LOCK

Preserve the real proportions of the garment.

Never:
- lengthen the body
- shorten the body
- widen the chest
- narrow the chest
- enlarge the shoulders
- shrink the shoulders
- lengthen sleeves
- shorten sleeves
- change sleeve opening
- exaggerate oversized fit
- turn regular fit into slim fit
- alter collar size
- alter neckline depth
- change placket length

The product must remain recognizably identical to the supplied garment.

---

## 7. SLEEVE LOCK

Sleeves are a critical identity feature.

For short-sleeve products:
- preserve the exact original sleeve length
- preserve the exact original sleeve width
- preserve the exact sleeve opening
- do not extend sleeves lower on the arm
- do not make sleeves wider or more oversized

When a prompt specifies sleeves straight and folded downward:
- both sleeves must fall downward
- sleeve fold lines must appear straight
- sleeves must not flare outward
- sleeves must not point sideways
- sleeves must not angle upward
- sleeves must not be artificially lengthened to achieve the downward position
- left and right sleeve length must remain equal
- left and right sleeve width must remain equal

Sleeve orientation may change only within the limits of the real garment construction.

---

## 8. LOGO / GRAPHIC / BRANDING LOCK — ABSOLUTE

Any genuine logo, artwork, embroidery, print or graphic visible on the supplied product must be preserved.

Maintain:
- exact placement
- relative scale
- orientation
- color
- shape
- line structure
- spacing
- visual hierarchy

Never:
- invent a replacement logo
- redraw a logo inaccurately
- omit existing branding
- blur branding
- move branding
- enlarge branding
- reduce branding
- stylize branding
- replace wording
- generate fake text

If the supplied branding cannot be reproduced accurately, prioritize faithful structure and placement rather than inventing new content.

---

## 9. LABEL LOCK

Preserve genuine garment labels when they are part of the requested view.

If instructed to remove loose threads from a label:
- remove only the loose visible threads
- keep the label itself
- keep its shape
- keep its placement
- keep its proportions
- keep its genuine branding
- do not redesign or replace the label

---

## 10. FABRIC LOCK

Preserve the actual material appearance.

Match:
- weave or knit structure
- texture
- thickness
- weight
- softness
- drape
- sheen
- density

Never:
- convert material type
- make matte fabric glossy
- make textured fabric smooth
- invent heavy grain
- add fake lint
- add random fibers
- over-sharpen fabric
- make the garment look plastic
- make cotton look synthetic

---

## 11. CONSTRUCTION LOCK

Preserve real construction details including:
- seams
- topstitching
- collar structure
- neck rib
- sleeve hems
- plackets
- button placement
- cuffs
- yokes
- side seams
- hem shape

Do not add construction elements that do not exist.

Do not remove genuine construction details.

---

## 12. GARMENT PREPARATION

Unless the source garment intentionally contains wrinkles as part of its design, present it as professionally prepared.

Preferred:
- steam-ironed
- clean
- smooth
- naturally shaped

Do not:
- erase all natural fabric behavior
- make the garment unnaturally flat
- create plastic-like surfaces
- introduce random deep wrinkles

---

## 13. BACKGROUND LOCK

If a prompt specifies the standard ENTITLED catalogue background, use:

- HEX #EDEBE8
- RGB 237, 235, 232
- sRGB

Maintain the same perceived shade across all catalogue images.

Do not:
- shift it warmer
- shift it cooler
- make it beige
- make it grey
- add gradients
- add texture
- add visible horizon lines unless explicitly requested

---

## 14. PRODUCT-IMAGE REFERENCE RULE

If an approved product image is explicitly supplied for direct embedding, use it unchanged.

Never:
- regenerate it
- redraw it
- recolor it
- reshape it
- retouch it
- alter branding
- change sleeves
- change collar
- change neckline
- alter proportions

This rule is especially important for graphics such as size charts.

---

## 15. SIZE-CHART RULE

When generating a size chart:

Measurement-source files are the sole source of truth for:
- sizes
- measurements
- units
- fit notation
- notes

Never:
- invent measurements
- round values
- modify units
- swap columns
- change size names
- infer missing values

A size-chart image is a measurement source only and must not be used as a garment-design reference unless explicitly stated.

---

## 16. MODEL IMAGE RULES

When placing the garment on a model:

Preserve the garment identity exactly.

The model must adapt to the garment.
The garment must not be redesigned to adapt to the model.

Do not:
- stretch logos
- distort graphics
- alter sleeve length
- change fit category
- reshape collar
- modify neckline
- hide defining garment features unnecessarily

Use natural fabric behavior appropriate to the real garment.

---

## 17. FOLDED PRODUCT RULES

When folding the product:

The fold may change presentation, but not garment identity.

Preserve:
- visible color
- fabric
- branding
- trims
- realistic thickness
- real proportions

Do not create:
- impossible folds
- duplicated sleeves
- missing hems
- extra layers
- fake stitching
- altered logos

---

## 18. MACRO IMAGE RULES

For fabric or construction macro images:

The macro must represent the actual garment material.

Do not invent:
- weave
- stitching
- fibers
- embroidery
- texture
- edge construction

Magnification must reveal genuine structure rather than create fictional detail.

---

## 19. REFERENCE IMAGE SEPARATION

When both product photographs and sample/reference images are supplied:

Product photographs control:
- garment identity
- product design
- color
- construction
- branding
- proportions
- material

Reference/sample images may control only:
- composition
- framing
- camera angle
- lighting direction
- posing
- general visual mood

Never transfer another garment's:
- sleeve length
- collar
- fit
- logo
- graphics
- fabric
- color
- construction

---

## 20. PREVIOUS GENERATION IS NOT A SOURCE

Never inherit garment details from an earlier AI-generated image unless it has been explicitly approved and supplied as the source for a specific task.

Each generation should return to the actual product photographs.

This prevents cumulative drift.

---

## 21. NO UNREQUESTED CREATIVE INTERPRETATION

Do not add:
- accessories
- patches
- pockets
- labels
- text
- embroidery
- piping
- patterns
- gradients
- distressing
- washes
- decorations
- extra seams
- fake texture

unless genuinely present or explicitly requested.

---

## 22. OUTPUT CLEANLINESS

Images must contain:
- no watermark
- no accidental text
- no fake labels
- no duplicated product elements
- no malformed buttons
- no floating threads unless genuinely present and intentionally retained
- no AI artifacts
- no border unless requested
- no collage unless requested

---

## 23. PRIORITY ORDER

For every generation, obey instructions in this order:

1. Actual product photographs
2. This ENTITLED_IMAGE_RULES.md file
3. ENTITLED_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md
4. Individual numbered prompt
5. Composition/sample reference

A lower-priority source must never override a higher-priority product-fidelity rule.

---

## 24. FINAL PRODUCT-FIDELITY CHECK

Before accepting an image, verify:

- correct garment category
- correct color
- correct undertone
- correct body length
- correct width
- correct sleeve length
- correct sleeve direction
- correct neckline/collar
- correct placket/buttons where applicable
- correct logo
- correct graphics
- correct label
- correct fabric texture
- correct seams
- no invented details
- no missing genuine details

If any identity-critical element differs materially from the supplied product, regenerate or correct the image.

---

## 25. CORE PRINCIPLE

The product is not a suggestion.

The product is the source of truth.

Art direction may change the world around the garment.

It may never change the garment itself.
